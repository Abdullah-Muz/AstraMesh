## 2. Analysis columns

**problem:** choose which satellites take part in a practical Byzantine fault-tolerance (pBFT) consensus round so that it completes soonest, given intermittent links between satellites with different orbits (§§I–III).

**agent_info:** 30 satellites from the International Charter: Space and Major Disasters (82 listed, reduced to the 30 with the most interactions over a day, Fig. 2), assumed to have onboard compute (§II-B).

**networking_assumptions:** a link exists when two satellites are within 500 km, with positions from PyEphem; 30 s time steps (§III-A, Eq. 2; §IV). No loss, bandwidth or latency model beyond range; the pBFT phases complete when every required message has been received (Eqs. 10–13).

**physics:** propagated orbits from catalogue elements (PyEphem); no perturbation model is discussed.

**fault_model:** Byzantine: pBFT tolerates f faulty replicas with r = 3f + 1, so the minimum round is 4 satellites (Eq. 1). No fault is simulated; the study measures time to consensus.

**safety_model:** none.

**method:** a MILP (solved with Gurobi) picks the primary and the other participants and schedules the four pBFT phases over the precomputed link windows, minimising completion time (Eqs. 5–14).

**results:** 200 instances on consecutive start times, 400 steps maximum (200 min); 100 were feasible, so consensus completed within 3.3 h "in half of cases studied" (abstract, §IV). Two satellites flying in formation (always in range) were chosen most often (Figs. 5–8).

**key_limitations:** idealised range-only links; no comparison with other consensus mechanisms or with non-consensus decisions; the 30-satellite pool is chosen by interaction count, which biases the result towards well-connected satellites.

**AstraMesh_overlap:**
- Evidence that agreement across a constellation with intermittent links can take hours: a G3 coordination rule should not need fleet-wide consensus before a spacecraft protects itself. This matches the spec (§6.4: local protection; spacecraft may rationally occupy different modes).
- Byzantine tolerance is out of AstraMesh's scope (no adversarial spacecraft); R04 is context, not a baseline.
