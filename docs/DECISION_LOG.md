| DL-026 | 2026-11-03 | **Open:** (a) whether the reference orbit's 525 km / 97.5° are mean or osculating elements; (b) how to initialize the formation so neighbours have equal mean a | **DECIDED 2026-11-03:** (a) **mean elements**; (b) **option 2**, numerical mean-a matching | **Measured** (`NUMERICAL_MISSION_DESIGN.md` §B.5): with equal osculating elements, the formation drifts 0.1–7.7 km per day depending on start angle. Read as osculating, 525/97.5 precesses 0.54% faster than sun-synchronous; read as mean, 0.04%. **Options for (b):** (1) analytic first-order J2 mean→osculating conversion (Kozai/Brouwer short-period terms); (2) numerical mean-a matching: adjust each spacecraft's initial osculating a until its one-orbit-averaged a equals the leader's, using the truth propagator; (3) keep equal osculating elements and restrict the start angle (fragile; not recommended). **Recommendation:** (a) mean elements; (b) option 2, with option 1 as a later cross-check. |
